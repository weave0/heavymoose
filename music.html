DROSS

Grief has a sound. This is it. The debut Heavy Moose record is ten tracks of industrial soundscapes built from digital grit — factory floors made audible, ember light translated into frequency. A figure in a wool overcoat standing in the crucible of what remains.

DROSS doesn't resolve. It documents. The slag left after the smelting — residue that turns out to be the most interesting material in the room.

Album 002

DROSS II

Twelve tracks forged in the aftermath. The second record picks up where DROSS left off — in the wreckage, yes, but also in the strange productivity of wreckage. Heavier water. Longer games. The work after the grief.

DROSS II is the construction phase. Still industrial, still dark, but oriented now toward what we build from what remains.
